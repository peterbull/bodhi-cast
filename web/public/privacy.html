Privacy

Updated October 17, 2026.

Your account

Google sign-in gives Bodhi Cast your Google account identifier, name, and email address. We use these to identify your account and show your name. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or calendar. Google access tokens are not retained.

Your spots

Your followed spots and custom locations are stored with your account. Custom locations are visible only while signed into that account. Coordinates are sent to NOAA to retrieve the relevant forecast; your name and email are not included.

Cookies and storage

A secure session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. A temporary cookie protects the sign-in process. Your measurement preference is stored in your browser. These are used for app functionality, not advertising.

Service providers and retention

The app and database run on DigitalOcean. Cloudflare handles traffic and security. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, and weather data comes from NOAA. These services receive connection information such as your IP address when your browser contacts them. Account data stays until you request its removal. Daily database backups rotate over seven days.

Contact

For privacy questions or account removal, contact the maintainer through the Bodhi Cast repository. Do not post private information in a public issue.
